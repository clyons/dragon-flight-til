Adapted for Dragon: Flight Playground by Ciaran Lyons, 2026. Changes: reduced 1,285,002 triangles to 120,106; grouped ten axial sections and their details into seven rigid bodies; kept the wings fixed to the shoulders; smoothed small body and wing ridges while preserving the head, feet and broad outline; scaled, reoriented and centred meshes; added approximate collision hulls and joint anchors. The source file and derived mesh hashes are recorded in `dragon.json`.
